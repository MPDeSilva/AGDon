Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD for ITSA) requires quarterly HMRC updates. It applies from 6 April 2026 if your income exceeds £50,000; April 2027 for over £30,000; April 2028 for over £20,000. I can get you fully prepared.
You must register for VAT when your taxable turnover exceeds £90,000 in any rolling 12-month period (unchanged since 1 April 2024). Voluntary registration below this threshold is also possible and sometimes beneficial.
